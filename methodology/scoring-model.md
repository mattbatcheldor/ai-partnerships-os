# Scoring methodology

## Purpose

The model is a transparent triage aid. It prioritises where further human qualification may be worthwhile; it does not predict revenue, supplier appetite or probability of closing.

## Account score

Each criterion is rated from 1 to 5. The weighted total is multiplied by 20 to produce a score out of 100.

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Need | 25% | Is there a problem the proposition could credibly solve? |
| Timing | 20% | Is there a current launch, scale-up or governance trigger? |
| Strategic fit | 20% | Is the Europe + expert + evaluation/data wedge relevant? |
| Expansion | 15% | Could a pilot compound into recurring work? |
| Access | 10% | Is there a credible route to the right buyer function? |
| Delivery feasibility | 10% | Can the first engagement be bounded and delivered credibly? |

Delivery feasibility is calculated as **6 − delivery complexity**.

## Tier rules

- **Tier 1:** score of 88 or more; immediate qualification or pilot-thesis work.
- **Tier 2:** score of 78–87; nurture, research or access-route building.
- **Watch:** below 78; retain the signal without forcing an opportunity.

## Human review

Every score should be challenged against five questions:

1. Does the source support the precise claim or only a broader signal?
2. Is the buyer function and entry offer specific without pretending to know internal priorities?
3. Is the expansion route linked to a recurring evaluation or data need?
4. Are famous companies being over-scored despite poor access or delivery feasibility?
5. Would a reader clearly distinguish sourced fact from author hypothesis?
