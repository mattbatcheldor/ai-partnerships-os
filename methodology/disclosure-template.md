# Reusable disclosure template

Use this structure across subsequent portfolio projects so that the evidence and simulation boundaries remain consistent.

## Short disclosure

> This is an independent portfolio project based on publicly available information. It was not commissioned by, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, **[case organisation]** or any company named. Public facts are source-linked; scores, partnership hypotheses, pilots and campaign materials are the author's analysis and simulations. Sources and AI outputs may contain errors or become outdated, so human verification is required before use or publication.

## Full boundary checklist

### Operating-system label

“Operating system” means a repeatable commercial workflow and decision framework. It does not mean deployed software, autonomous operation or proprietary technology unless a working system is separately demonstrated.

### Evidence label

Named companies and cited market signals come from public sources. Public relevance does not prove internal need, budget, procurement intent or supplier appetite.

### Hypothesis label

Prioritisation scores, buyer problems, partnership wedges, entry offers and expansion paths are author hypotheses for testing—not company facts or opportunity probabilities.

### Simulation label

Campaign messages, outreach plans, conversion assumptions and scenarios are fictional simulations unless explicitly supported by execution evidence. Do not imply that messages were sent, replies received or results achieved.

### AI and human-review label

AI may assist research structuring, drafting, evaluation and comparison. AI can omit, distort or invent information. A human remains responsible for source verification, judgement, editing and publication.

### Results label

Model-evaluation results describe the documented test only. They do not establish universal model performance, commercial outcomes or scientific validity.

### Freshness label

Record a verification date and re-check all material claims before an application, interview, external presentation or publication.
