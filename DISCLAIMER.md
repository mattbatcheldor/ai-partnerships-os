# Disclaimer and publication boundary

This is an independent portfolio project created by Matthew Batcheldor using publicly available information. It was inspired by a public Partnerships & Growth role brief, but was not commissioned by, and is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by Sovrano or any company named.

The phrase **“partnerships operating system”** describes a repeatable commercial workflow and decision framework. It does not mean a deployed software platform, autonomous agent or claim of proprietary technology.

Public facts are paraphrased and linked to official sources. Partnership wedges, prioritisation scores, entry offers, campaigns and expansion routes are the author's hypotheses and simulations. They do not establish buying intent, supplier appetite, commercial engagement or delivery commitment.

The evaluation contains actual first-pass model outputs from one documented session, followed by provisional self-adjudication. It does not establish universal model performance and should not be presented as an independent scientific benchmark.

## Known scope for error

- Public information may be incomplete, outdated, incorrectly interpreted or removed after verification.
- A visible market signal may be relevant without establishing an internal problem, budget, buying intention or route to procurement.
- AI-generated outputs may omit, distort or invent information, even when prompts include evidence constraints.
- Scores, weights and tiers reflect author judgement and may contain confirmation, selection or brand-recognition bias.
- Illustrative pilots simplify operational, technical, contractual, legal, privacy, consent, safety and delivery requirements.
- Model-evaluation results come from a small, single-session test with provisional self-adjudication.
- No outreach, buyer validation, commercial pipeline, revenue or delivery outcome is claimed.
