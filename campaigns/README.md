# Partnership campaign simulations

These are fictional portfolio exercises. No message was sent, no response was received and no commercial result is claimed.

## Campaign F01 — European Expert Evals

- **Audience:** evaluation, research partnerships, safety and applied-AI leaders.
- **Hypothesis:** a domain-specific problem statement will outperform generic multilingual-evaluation language.
- **Entry asset:** a sample failure taxonomy or bounded evaluation design.
- **Primary CTA:** test whether one language/domain gap exists before proposing a programme.
- **Watch-out:** position independent experts as complementary to internal teams.

## Campaign R01 — Expert Task Intelligence

- **Audience:** robot learning, world-model/data, fleet operations and research partnerships.
- **Hypothesis:** desktop-first task design will reduce concern about immediate physical-delivery complexity.
- **Entry asset:** task map, edge-case catalogue, acceptance rubric and capture specification.
- **Primary CTA:** validate task and success criteria before investing in another data batch.
- **Watch-out:** no physical capture is promised until hardware, consent, rights and safety requirements are agreed.

## Campaign E01 — Independent Enterprise Assurance

- **Audience:** group AI, model risk, compliance/legal, business owners and procurement.
- **Hypothesis:** independent evidence and synthetic-data framing will outperform broad compliance language.
- **Entry asset:** fixed-scope evaluation of one workflow using synthetic or authorised cases.
- **Primary CTA:** strengthen a release or governance decision without exposing production data.
- **Watch-out:** the output is decision support, not legal certification.

## Experiment design

Each campaign contains three A/B hypotheses, a primary metric, a minimum comparable sample and a decision rule. The framework deliberately distinguishes **planned conversion assumptions** from achieved results.
